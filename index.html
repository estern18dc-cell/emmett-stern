LLM evaluation · Independent · 2026

US–China military knowledge-withholding eval

An evaluation testing whether frontier models withhold what they know about the US and Chinese militaries, and whether US-lab and Chinese-lab models do so symmetrically. Built on 70 matched question pairs and 228 verified facts, asked in English and Simplified Chinese across eight labs.

Role: Designed the dataset, pipeline, and pre-registration

Results forthcoming

I lead the framework paper and the wargaming and escalation module: what wargames with AI players actually measure, and why lost decision time and override authority may be the larger risk.

Simulation · Personal project · 2026

WARGAME: a Monte Carlo portfolio wargame

A browser-based strategy game that treats investing like a campaign. Markets move through a six-regime Markov chain calibrated to S&P history; players issue multi-year plans and if-then orders, draw event cards, and every move is scored against holding steady on the same simulated futures, after tax.

Role: Designed and built it (JavaScript, Python)

Play the demo (fictional $10M portfolio)
